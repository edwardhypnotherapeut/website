## Before the first session

You do not need to prepare anything. A short description of what is going on is enough for the intake call. If you have relevant medical information or are receiving treatment elsewhere, it helps to mention it — but it is not required to start.

## The intake call

Twenty minutes by phone or video. You describe what is going on; I ask questions, explain the options and am honest about what I can and cannot help with. If another form of care would serve you better, I will say so.

There is no charge for this call and no pressure to book.

## The first session

We take time to understand the context, agree on a goal and decide on the approach. You will experience what focused attention feels like, and leave with a clearer plan. The first session is seventy-five minutes.

## Between sessions

Change happens in daily life, not only in the room. You will usually leave with one or two small practices or observations. They are designed to be manageable — not another task to fail at.

## Ending well

We review progress every few sessions. When the goal is reached, we stop. If something new emerges, you are welcome to return. There is no package that ties you in and no expectation to continue longer than is useful.

## Practical notes

Sessions take place in Vlaardingen, a short drive from Rotterdam, or online. The practice is discreet and easily reached by car or public transport. Payment is made after the session by bank transfer or card.
