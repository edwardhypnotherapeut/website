## Questions that go deeper

Soul regression is a contemplative form of regression work. It is chosen by people who sense that a difficulty is not only a personal history, but touches something larger: a life theme, a sense of purpose, a question about what they carry.

We work with deep relaxation and guided imagery, allowing images and impressions to arise without forcing an interpretation. Some people experience vivid scenes; others work more quietly, through symbols or bodily sensations. Both are valid. Nothing needs to be proven for the process to be meaningful.

## What it can help with

- existential questions and loss of direction
- a persistent sense of not belonging
- grief with a spiritual dimension
- patterns that resist ordinary therapy
- the search for meaning after a turning point

## What a session looks like

We begin by clarifying your question. The regression itself is longer and more spacious than a standard session, with room for silence. Afterwards we take time to ground, reflect and translate the experience into something usable in daily life.

Sessions last two hours. You do not need any prior experience with meditation or regression. A willingness to be curious is enough.

## An honest frame

This work is offered alongside, not instead of, regular medical or psychological care. I will not present images as facts or memories as proof. What arises is treated as material to explore — something that may help you relate differently to your life, not a claim about the universe.
