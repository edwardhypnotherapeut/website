## Does this sound familiar?

You are exhausted all day and wide awake at night. Your body settles, your mind starts. Some nights you fall asleep and wake at three, immediately alert. Other nights you lie there doing the maths on how many hours are left.

Over time, bedtime itself becomes charged. You start to dread the bedroom, which makes sleep even less likely.

## What is often underneath

Insomnia is rarely solved by sleep hygiene alone, although the basics matter. More often the mind stays awake because it is holding something: an unresolved conversation, a vigilance that will not stand down, a belief that rest must be earned.

For some people, the pattern began during a stressful period and simply never switched off. The body learned that night is for monitoring, not resting.

## How hypnotherapy can help

We work on down-regulation: teaching the nervous system, in a relaxed state, that it is safe to let go. Guided imagery, slow breathing and suggestion can interrupt the alertness loop and reconnect bedtime with calm rather than performance.

We also look at the thoughts that arrive at 3 a.m. — often the same ones every night — and give you something more useful to do with them. If there is an underlying worry or grief, we address that too, rather than only the symptom.

## A realistic expectation

Most people notice improvement within two to four sessions. Long-standing insomnia may need more, and if a medical cause is suspected I will refer you to your GP first.
