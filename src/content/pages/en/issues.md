## Most people arrive without a neat label

They describe a feeling: tension that will not lift, a pattern in relationships, a mind that will not switch off at night. The pages below describe the themes I work with most often, but they are starting points rather than categories. Two people with the same complaint may need very different work.

Therapy here is short-term and goal-oriented by default, though longer trajectories are possible when the material calls for it. We review progress regularly, and you are free to stop at any point — no explanation required.

## When this is not the right help

Hypnotherapy is not crisis care. If you are in acute danger or need urgent psychiatric help, contact your GP, the Dutch GP out-of-hours service, or emergency services on 112. For confidential support, you can also contact MIND Korrelatie or 113 Suicide Prevention.

If your situation requires specialist medical or psychiatric treatment, I will say so. That is not a rejection; it is part of careful practice.
