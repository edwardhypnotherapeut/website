## Who is responsible

Edward Hazebroek Hypnotherapy, Stoomloggerweg 8, 3133 KT Vlaardingen, the Netherlands, is responsible for the processing of personal data described here. You can reach the practice at info@edwardhazebroek.nl or +31 6 34 04 04 03.

## What data is processed

When you contact the practice or start a trajectory, the following may be recorded:

- name, email address, phone number and any message you send
- appointment details and correspondence
- notes from sessions, kept to a minimum and focused on the treatment
- information you choose to share about your health or background
- invoicing details, where applicable

The website itself does not use tracking cookies or analytics that identify individual visitors.

## Why data is processed

Personal data is used to:

- respond to your enquiry and schedule appointments
- provide safe and continuous care, including preparation and follow-up
- keep necessary records and issue invoices
- comply with legal and professional obligations

## Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent, on the performance of an agreement for care, and on legal obligations that apply to a healthcare practice. Where health data is involved, processing takes place under the explicit consent you give before a trajectory begins.

## How long data is kept

Contact details and correspondence are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry. Session notes are kept for the period required by professional and legal retention rules, and are then destroyed. Invoices are kept for the statutory period.

## Sharing

Data is not sold or shared for marketing. Information is only shared with third parties when necessary — for example with your GP or another practitioner, and only with your written permission — or when the law requires it. Service providers such as the booking system or email host process data on behalf of the practice under an agreement.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- request access to the data held about you
- ask for correction or completion
- ask for deletion where retention is no longer required
- withdraw consent for future processing
- object to processing or request a restriction
- receive your data in a portable format

Requests can be sent to info@edwardhazebroek.nl. A response follows within thirty days.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures protect personal data, including restricted access, secure devices and encrypted connections. Paper notes are stored in a locked space.

## Complaints

If you believe your data has not been handled correctly, please raise it with the practice first. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the practice or legislation changes. The most recent version is always published on this page.
