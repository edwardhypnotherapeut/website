## A note on reading testimonials

These accounts are personal and anonymised. They describe what individuals experienced, not a guarantee of outcome. Therapy depends on many factors — the question you bring, the fit with your therapist, timing and your own involvement.

If a particular story resonates, that is useful information. Bring it to the intake call and we can talk about what it points to in your situation.
