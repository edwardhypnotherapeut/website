## Included in every session

Every rate covers preparation, the session itself and a brief written follow-up with the main points and any practices agreed. There are no separate intake fees and no registration costs.

## Packages

A discount is available when you book three sessions in advance: ten percent off the total. This is optional. Many people prefer to decide session by session, and that is perfectly fine.

## Payment

Payment is made after the session by bank transfer or card. You will receive an invoice for your records. Rates are reviewed annually; the price agreed at the start of a trajectory is held for its duration.

## Cancellations

Appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours in advance. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged at the full session rate. If something urgent happens, contact me and we will find a fair solution.

## Insurance

Hypnotherapy is not usually reimbursed by Dutch health insurers, and it falls outside the basic package. Some supplementary policies cover alternative or complementary care; check your policy and any conditions before starting. I can provide an invoice with the necessary details.

## VAT and registration

Rates include VAT where applicable. Practice registration and professional body details are listed in the footer and on the contact page.
