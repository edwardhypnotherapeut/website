## Every approach starts with the same assumption

Your symptoms made sense at some point, even if they no longer serve you. The differences between these therapies lie in how we reach that material — through focused attention, through memory, through the wider system, or through questions of meaning.

Hypnotherapy works with the beliefs and habits that sit just below ordinary awareness. Regression therapy follows a present reaction back to where it began. Constellation work looks at the family system: loyalties, entanglements and unspoken rules that shape behaviour. Soul regression sits at the reflective, existential end — questions about direction, purpose and what you may be carrying that is not yours.

Most trajectories combine elements. We decide together, and we adjust as we go. You will never be asked to accept an explanation that does not fit your experience.

If you already know which approach appeals to you, each page explains the process, the length of a session and what it is — and is not — suitable for. If not, start with a conversation.
