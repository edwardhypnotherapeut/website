## Does this sound familiar?

You rehearse conversations before they happen and replay them afterwards. A casual remark stays with you for days. You avoid situations where you might be judged — a meeting, a party, a phone call — and then feel disappointed in yourself for avoiding them.

Social anxiety is more than shyness. It is a learned alarm system that treats ordinary exposure as danger, and it can shrink your world gradually enough that you barely notice.

## How hypnotherapy can help

In a relaxed, focused state we work with the beliefs underneath the alarm: that you will be exposed, that everyone is watching, that a mistake is permanent. We rehearse new responses imaginatively and practise small, real-world steps between sessions.

The aim is not to make you extroverted. It is to give you back your range of movement — so that saying yes or no becomes a choice rather than a reflex.

## What we might work on

- the physical surge before speaking
- over-preparing and over-apologising
- the inner critic that reviews every interaction
- avoidance that has quietly become a habit
