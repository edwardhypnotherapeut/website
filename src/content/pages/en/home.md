## Therapy for the part of you that already knows something needs to change

You may have tried to think your way out of it. Read the books, made the plans, promised yourself that this time would be different. And still the same pattern returns — at work, in relationships, at three in the morning.

Hypnotherapy works with the deeper layer where those patterns live. Not by switching you off or taking over, but by helping your attention settle long enough for something new to become possible. It is a focused, collaborative process: you stay awake, you stay in charge, and you decide what we work on.

At the practice in Vlaardingen — a short drive from Rotterdam — I combine hypnotherapy with regression therapy and systemic constellation work. Some people arrive with a clear goal. Others come with a feeling they cannot quite name. Both are a good place to start.

If you are unsure whether this is right for you, book the free twenty-minute intake. No preparation, no commitment — just a conversation.
