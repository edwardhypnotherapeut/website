## More than tiredness

Burnout is not simply being tired. It is a state of depletion in which rest no longer restores you, cynicism creeps in, and the things you used to care about feel distant. You may keep functioning — many people do — while feeling hollow underneath.

The body often speaks first: sleep that does not repair, tension, headaches, a lowered tolerance for noise and demands.

## The story underneath

Time off is necessary, but on its own it rarely changes the pattern. Many people return to the same workload, the same standards, the same inability to say no, and find themselves back where they started within months.

Common drivers include:

- worth tied to achievement
- responsibility for everything and everyone
- a nervous system that cannot fully stand down
- a belief that stopping is dangerous

## Recovery is not a sprint

We work at a pace that does not reproduce the pressure that caused the problem. Hypnotherapy helps down-regulate the alert system and restore a sense of safety in stillness. We also examine the beliefs that make rest feel risky, and design boundaries that are realistic rather than heroic.

Regression work, where relevant, can trace how far back the pattern of over-responsibility goes.

## What recovery looks like

Not a sudden return to the old self, but a more sustainable version of it: energy that lasts, clearer limits, and the ability to stop before your body stops you.
