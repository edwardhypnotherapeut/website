## What to include

A short message is enough: what is going on, what you would like help with, and whether you prefer an in-person or online session. You do not need to share more than feels comfortable in writing.

If you already have a preference for a particular therapy, mention it. If not, we will figure it out together.

## Practical information

The practice is at Stoomloggerweg 8 in Vlaardingen, about fifteen minutes from central Rotterdam by car. There is free parking nearby, and the practice is easily reached by public transport. The entrance is discreet and the waiting area is private.

Sessions are available in English and Dutch. Online sessions take place via a secure video link; you will receive the details after booking.

## If you are in crisis

This website and contact form are not suitable for urgent situations. If you are in acute danger, contact emergency services on 112. For urgent psychological help, contact your GP or the Dutch GP out-of-hours service. For confidential support, MIND Korrelatie and 113 Suicide Prevention are available.

## Response time

I answer messages personally, usually within one working day. If you have not heard back within two working days, please check your spam folder or send a WhatsApp message.
