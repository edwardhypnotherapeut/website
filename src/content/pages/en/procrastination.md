## Not laziness

You care, possibly too much. The task matters, which is exactly why it feels heavy. You wait for the right moment, the right mood, a cleaner desk — and the waiting produces its own low hum of guilt.

Procrastination is a coping strategy, not a character flaw. It offers short-term relief from a discomfort that has not yet been named.

## What the delay protects

Common layers underneath:

- fear of doing it badly, so not starting feels safer than trying
- a perfectionist standard that makes any first draft unacceptable
- resentment about being controlled or evaluated
- overwhelm, when the task is really several tasks in one

When the underlying feeling is addressed, the delay often loses its grip without a fight.

## How hypnotherapy helps

We work with the belief that keeps the task dangerous — that the result defines you, that mistakes are permanent, that you must feel ready before you begin. In a calm, focused state we rehearse starting imperfectly and tolerating the discomfort that follows.

Between sessions we design small, specific experiments. Not another productivity system, but evidence that you can begin before you feel like it.
