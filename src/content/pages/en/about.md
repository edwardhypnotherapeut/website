## A practical approach

I came to this work through an interest in how people change — not through drama, but through small, precise shifts in attention. Hypnotherapy appealed to me because it respects both the body and the mind, and because it asks for collaboration rather than surrender.

In my practice you will not find incense, promises of miracles or a guru. You will find a quiet room, clear questions and a method that treats you as the expert on your own life.

## Background

I trained as a clinical hypnotherapist and later specialised in regression therapy and systemic constellation work. Over the past twelve years I have guided more than nine hundred sessions with adults facing anxiety, sleep problems, burnout, grief and repeating relationship patterns.

My education continues: supervision, intervision and further training are part of the practice, not an afterthought. I work within a professional code of conduct and refer to medical or psychiatric care whenever that is the better route.

## How I work

Sessions are structured but not rigid. We always start with what is present, agree on what today is for, and end with something usable. I ask direct questions, listen carefully, and I will tell you when I do not know.

I work in English and Dutch. Online sessions are available for hypnotherapy and regression; constellations take place in person.

## Outside the practice

When I am not working I am usually outside — walking, cycling, or failing to keep a vegetable garden in order. That practical, unhurried rhythm finds its way into the practice too.
