## The loop

You recognise it afterwards, almost from outside: the withdrawal, the sharp reply, the sudden need to fix everything, the collapse into silence. In the moment it feels like the only option. Later you wonder why it happened again.

Emotional patterns are not random. They are efficient responses that once protected you, rehearsed until they became automatic.

## What patterns do

A pattern usually carries a belief and a strategy:

- “If I show need, I will be too much” → manage alone, then resent the distance
- “If I am not perfect, I will be rejected” → overwork, then collapse
- “Conflict means danger” → appease, then feel invisible
- “I must stay in control” → plan everything, then feel exhausted

The strategy makes sense. The cost is what brings people to therapy.

## How therapy helps

Hypnotherapy works well with the moment before the reaction — the half-second in which the body already knows what is coming. We slow that moment down, explore what it expects, and rehearse a response that fits the present rather than the past.

Regression can show where the pattern was learned. Constellation work can reveal how it belongs to the wider family system. Often more than one layer is involved.

## What we work on

- noticing the trigger before the reaction
- naming the feeling without being overtaken by it
- experimenting with new responses in low-risk situations
- repairing ruptures rather than avoiding them
