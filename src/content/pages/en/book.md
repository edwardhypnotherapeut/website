## What happens after you book

You will receive a confirmation by email with the time, location or video link, and a short note on how to prepare. There is nothing to study and no form to complete in advance.

The intake call is a conversation, not an assessment. You can decide afterwards whether you want to continue, and there is no charge for the call itself.

## Rescheduling

Plans change. Appointments can be moved free of charge up to 24 hours in advance. Later cancellations are charged at the full session rate, so please let me know as early as possible.

## Prefer to talk first?

Use the form to ask a question or request a call back. You can also send a WhatsApp message during practice hours. I answer personally, usually within one working day.

## Before your first session

Bring whatever you would like to discuss. If you have relevant medical information, a referral letter or are receiving treatment elsewhere, you are welcome to mention it. You do not need to prepare anything else.
